# High Availability and Multi-Region Architecture

**Maturity:** Design

Dits has no hosted control plane, multi-region service, replicated database, failover system, durability SLA, or production availability target. The former topology and operating procedures were architectural exploration.

See [`../STATUS.md`](../STATUS.md), the
[active architecture](active-architecture.md), and
[core concepts](../concepts.md). Detailed formats remain implementation contracts until
an accepted ADR and conformance corpus make them public.
