# Service-Level Status

**Maturity:** Current

Dits does not currently operate a hosted product and does not offer a binding service
level agreement.

There are no guaranteed:

- uptime or availability percentages;
- durability, backup, recovery-point, or recovery-time objectives;
- API, storage, transfer, or processing performance targets;
- support response or resolution times; or
- service credits, subscription tiers, or remedies for missed targets.

The local alpha is provided under the repository license. Maintainer help, fixes, and
releases are best effort and are not a support contract. Targets or examples in design
documents, tests, and benchmarks are non-binding and apply only to the conditions they
explicitly describe.

A future SLA would require a real service, measured service-level indicators,
production monitoring, tested incident and recovery procedures, defined support
coverage, and counsel-reviewed commercial terms. No SLA claim should be published
until those prerequisites exist.

[`../../SECURITY.md`](../../SECURITY.md) for the security-reporting policy.
