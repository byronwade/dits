# Compliance Status

**Maturity:** Current

Dits is open-source, local-first alpha software. The project does not currently
operate a hosted service or process customer data on a customer's behalf. Software
features alone do not make a user's deployment compliant with any law, regulation, or
industry standard.

## Current status

Dits does not currently provide or claim:

- SOC 2, ISO 27001, PCI DSS, or other independent certification or attestation;
- HIPAA eligibility or a Business Associate Agreement;
- a Data Processing Agreement or Standard Contractual Clauses;
- GDPR, CCPA, LGPD, FERPA, or industry-specific service compliance;
- audited operational controls, designated data regions, or a subprocessor program; or
- a hosted identity, billing, support, retention, backup, or incident-response service.

Users are responsible for evaluating and operating their own installation, protecting
their data, managing access, maintaining backups, and meeting any obligations that
apply to their deployment. Dits must not be described as certified, compliant, or
enterprise-ready on the basis of this repository.

## Gate for future claims

A future hosted or regulated offering would require, at minimum, an identified legal
operator, counsel-reviewed terms, a data-flow and subprocessor inventory, documented
security and privacy controls, incident and recovery processes, contractual materials,
and relevant independent audits. Until those systems and evidence exist, any such
capability remains a proposal rather than a product commitment.
